After confirming your appointment schedule, the system will direct you to the payment section.

Payment can usually be made through:
- Authorized payment centers

- Mobile payment services

- Regular Processing
- Expedited Processing

Before your scheduled appointment, prepare all required documents to avoid delays. These typically include:

- Confirmed appointment packet
- Printed application form
- Valid government-issued ID
- Supporting documents (such as birth certificate or previous passport)

Check the DFA website for the latest list of required documents depending on whether you are applying for a **new passport** or **renewal**.

Arrive at the DFA office at least 30 minutes before your appointment schedule. Bring all required documents and your payment confirmation.

- Document verification
- Data encoding
- Biometrics (photo, fingerprints, and signature capture)

After completing biometrics, you will be given instructions regarding the release of your passport.

- Courier delivery to your address
- Personal pickup at the DFA office
