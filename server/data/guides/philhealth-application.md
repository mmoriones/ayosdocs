## 1. Register as a PhilHealth Member
To apply for a PhilHealth ID, you must first be registered as a member of the Philippine Health Insurance Corporation (PhilHealth).

You may register through the following methods:

- **Online Registration** – Visit the official PhilHealth website and complete the membership registration form.
- **Walk-in Registration** – Go to the nearest PhilHealth office and request a PhilHealth Member Registration Form (PMRF).

During registration, you will be asked to provide personal information such as:

- Full name
- Birthdate and birthplace
- Address
- Contact information
- Civil status
- Employment information (if applicable)

Make sure that the details you provide are accurate and match your valid identification documents.

## 2. Fill Out the PhilHealth Member Registration Form (PMRF)
The PMRF is the official document used to register or update your membership information.

When completing the form, make sure to:

- Write clearly and legibly
- Double check spelling of names and personal details
- Provide correct contact information
- Indicate your membership category (employed, self-employed, indigent, senior citizen, etc.)

If you are already registered but need to update your details, you can also use the PMRF for updating your information.

## 3. Prepare the Required Documents
Before submitting your application, prepare the required supporting documents.

These may include:

- At least one valid government-issued ID
- Birth certificate (for new members if required)
- Additional documents depending on membership type

Examples of valid IDs include:

- National ID
- Driver’s License
- Passport
- Postal ID
- Voter’s ID

It is recommended to bring both the **original copy and a photocopy** of your documents.

## 4. Submit Your Application
Submit the completed PMRF form together with your supporting documents at the nearest PhilHealth office.

The staff will review your application and verify the information you provided. If everything is complete, your membership will be processed.

Once approved, you will be issued your **PhilHealth Identification Number (PIN)** which serves as your permanent membership number.

Keep this number safe because it will be used for all PhilHealth transactions.

## 5. Receive Your PhilHealth ID
After your membership has been successfully registered, you can request a **PhilHealth Identification Card (PIC)**.

The PhilHealth ID is typically issued by the local PhilHealth office and contains your:

- Full name
- PhilHealth Identification Number
- Signature
- Photograph (for some versions)

This ID can be used when accessing PhilHealth services and benefits.

## 6. Use Your PhilHealth Membership
Once you receive your PhilHealth number and ID, you can begin using your benefits when accessing healthcare services.

PhilHealth helps cover part of the costs for:

- Hospitalization
- Certain medical procedures
- Select outpatient services

Always bring your PhilHealth number or ID when visiting accredited hospitals or clinics so your benefits can be properly applied.
