## 1. What is a Passport Appointment?

A **passport appointment** is a scheduled booking required before you can apply for or renew a Philippine passport.

* It is an online reservation system  
* It exists to manage applicant flow and avoid long queues  
* It is handled by the Department of Foreign Affairs (DFA)  

You must secure an appointment before visiting any DFA passport processing site.

---

## 2. Who Needs This?

You need a passport appointment if you are:

- First-time passport applicants  
- Individuals renewing their passport  
- Minors applying for a passport  
- Adults with lost or damaged passports  

---

## 3. Requirements

### Primary Requirements

* Confirmed appointment packet  
* Printed application form  
* Valid government-issued ID  

### Additional Requirements (if applicable)

* PSA birth certificate  
* Old passport (for renewal)  
* Supporting documents (for name change, lost passport, etc.)  

💡 Tip: Make sure documents are valid and readable.

---

## 4. Step-by-Step Process

### Step 1 – Register an Online Account

Go to the DFA Passport Appointment website and create an account using your email.

Enter accurate personal details. A confirmation email will be sent to proceed.

---

### Step 2 – Schedule Your Appointment

Log in and select your preferred DFA office or site.

Choose an available date and time. Double-check your information before confirming.

---

### Step 3 – Pay the Passport Fee

Pay through:

- Authorized payment centers  
- Online banking  
- Mobile wallets  

Keep your receipt or reference number.

---

### Step 4 – Attend Your Appointment

Arrive at least 30 minutes early with all documents.

Your application will go through:

* Document verification  
* Data encoding  
* Biometrics capture  

---

### Step 5 – Claim or Receive Passport

Choose between:

- Courier delivery  
- Pickup at DFA office  

Keep your claim stub or tracking number.

---

---

## 6. Processing Time

- Standard processing: 7–12 working days  
- Possible delays: Peak seasons or incomplete documents  

---

## 7. Common Mistakes to Avoid

* Incorrect personal information  
* Missing documents  
* Late arrival on appointment date  
* Losing payment receipt  

---

## 8. Tips for Faster Processing

- Book early (slots fill quickly)  
- Prepare documents in advance  
- Arrive early at the DFA office  
- Double-check all entries before submission

## 10. Frequently Asked Questions

### Can I walk in without an appointment?

No, DFA requires an appointment for most applicants.

### Can I reschedule my appointment?

Yes, depending on DFA policies and availability of slots.

---

## 11. Summary

Booking a passport appointment is required before applying or renewing your passport. Create an account, schedule your visit, pay the fee, and attend your appointment with complete documents. After processing, you can choose delivery or pickup of your passport.
