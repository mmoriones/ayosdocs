## 1. What is DTI Business Name Registration?

DTI Business Name Registration is the process of registering a business name for a sole proprietorship with the Department of Trade and Industry (DTI) in the Philippines.

This registration grants the owner the right to use the chosen business name for commercial activities within a specified territorial scope.

The DTI Business Name Registration is commonly used for:
* Starting a sole proprietorship business
* Applying for business permits and BIR registration
* Opening business bank accounts

---

## 2. Who Needs This?

You may need DTI Business Name Registration if you are:
- Starting a sole proprietorship business
- Operating a small business or online shop
- Applying for local business permits
- Registering with the BIR as a business owner

---

## 3. Benefits of Having DTI Business Name Registration

### Legal Right to Use a Business Name
The registration gives the owner authority to use the approved business name within the selected territorial scope.

### Requirement for Other Business Permits
Most LGUs and government agencies require DTI registration before issuing permits and tax registrations.

### Builds Business Credibility
A registered business name improves trust with customers, suppliers, and financial institutions.

---

## 4. Requirements

### Primary Requirements
* Valid government-issued ID
* Proposed business name
* Personal information of the applicant
* Active email address and mobile number

### Additional Requirements (if applicable)
* Barangay clearance
* Proof of business address
* Authorization letter for representatives

💡 Tip: Prepare at least 3–5 alternative business names in case your preferred name is unavailable or restricted.

---

## 5. Application Methods

Applicants may register through online systems or selected DTI offices.

### Online Application
Applicants may register online through the DTI Business Name Registration System (BNRS).

The online portal allows:
* Business name search
* Online application submission
* Fee payment
* Certificate download

### Walk-In Application
Applicants may also visit:
* DTI Regional Offices
* DTI Provincial Offices
* Negosyo Centers

Some offices may require appointments.

---

## 6. Step-by-Step Application Process

### Step 1 – Prepare Your Business Information
Prepare your:
* Proposed business names
* Business address
* Business activity details
* Valid IDs and contact information

---

### Step 2 – Check Business Name Availability
Use the DTI BNRS portal or inquire at DTI offices to verify if your preferred business name is available.

DTI may reject names that are:
* Identical or confusingly similar to existing businesses
* Offensive or misleading
* Restricted by law

---

### Step 3 – Submit the Application
Complete the application form online or at the DTI office.

Select your territorial scope:
* Barangay
* City/Municipality
* Regional
* National

---

### Step 4 – Pay the Registration Fees
Pay through:
* GCash
* Maya
* Debit/Credit Card
* LandBank
* Over-the-counter payment centers

---

### Step 5 – Receive Your Certificate
After approval and payment confirmation:
* Online applicants may download the certificate digitally
* Walk-in applicants may receive printed copies

---

## 14. Frequently Asked Questions

### Is DTI registration required for all businesses?
DTI registration is generally required for sole proprietorship businesses using a business name other than the owner's legal name.

### How long is DTI Business Name Registration valid?
The registration is generally valid for 5 years unless revoked or canceled.

### Can I renew my DTI registration online?
Yes. DTI allows online renewal through the BNRS portal.

### Is DTI registration the same as a business permit?
No. DTI registration only registers the business name. You still need:
* Barangay Clearance
* Mayor's Permit
* BIR Registration
* Other permits if applicable

### Can online sellers register with DTI?
Yes. Many online businesses and sellers register their business names with DTI.
