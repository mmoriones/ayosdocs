## 1. What is Marriage License?

A Marriage License is an official legal document issued by the Local Government Unit (LGU) through the Local Civil Registrar (LCR), authorizing two individuals to marry in the Philippines.

Under Philippine law, most couples must obtain a marriage license before a civil or church wedding can legally proceed.

The Marriage License is commonly used for:
* Civil weddings
* Church weddings
* Legal registration of marriage

---

## 2. Who Needs This?

You may need a Marriage License if you are:
- Planning to get married in the Philippines
- Applying for a church or civil wedding
- Registering a legal union recognized by Philippine law

---

## 3. Benefits of Having Marriage License

### Legal Authority to Marry
The license legally authorizes a couple to marry anywhere in the Philippines within its validity period.

### Required for Wedding Ceremonies
Churches, judges, mayors, and solemnizing officers usually require a valid marriage license before officiating a wedding.

### Enables Official Marriage Registration
Without a valid marriage license, most marriages cannot be properly registered with the PSA.

---

## 4. Requirements

### Primary Requirements
* PSA Birth Certificate of both applicants
* PSA CENOMAR of both applicants
* Valid government-issued IDs
* Community Tax Certificate (Cedula)
* Marriage License Application Form

### Additional Requirements (if applicable)
* Parental Consent (if either applicant is 18–20 years old)
* Parental Advice (if either applicant is 21–24 years old)
* Certificate of Attendance from Pre-Marriage Counseling
* Certificate of No Marriage from foreign embassy (for foreigners)
* Death Certificate or Annulment Papers for previously married applicants

💡 Tip: Some LGUs may have additional local requirements. Always verify with your Local Civil Registrar before applying.

---

## 5. Application Methods

Marriage License applications are generally processed through walk-in applications at the Local Civil Registrar (LCR) office of the city or municipality where either applicant resides.

### Online Application
Some LGUs provide online appointment systems or downloadable application forms through their official websites.

However, personal appearance is usually still required for verification and seminar attendance.

### Walk-In Application
Applicants may visit the Local Civil Registrar office directly to submit requirements and process their application.

Both applicants are usually required to appear in person.

---

## 6. Step-by-Step Application Process

### Step 1 – Secure PSA Documents
Obtain updated PSA Birth Certificates and PSA CENOMAR copies for both applicants.

---

### Step 2 – Attend Required Seminars
Attend mandatory seminars such as:
* Pre-Marriage Counseling
* Family Planning Seminar
* Responsible Parenthood Seminar

Requirements vary depending on the LGU.

---

### Step 3 – Submit Application Forms
Submit completed application forms along with all documentary requirements to the Local Civil Registrar.

The officer will review and verify the submitted documents.

---

### Step 4 – Pay the Applicable Fees
Pay the required processing fees at the LGU cashier or authorized payment center.

Keep all official receipts.

---

### Step 5 – Wait for the 10-Day Publication Period
Philippine law requires the marriage license application to be posted publicly for 10 consecutive days.

This allows any legal objections to be raised.

---

### Step 6 – Claim the Marriage License
After the publication period and approval, applicants may claim their Marriage License from the Local Civil Registrar.

The license is generally valid for 120 days nationwide.

---

## 7. Where to Apply

Applicants may process their Marriage License at:
- Local Civil Registrar Office (City Hall or Municipal Hall)
- LGU Civil Registry Department
- Selected LGU online appointment portals

Applications are usually filed in the city or municipality where either applicant resides.

## 9. Processing Time

Typical processing timelines include:
* Document preparation: 1–7 days
* Seminar attendance: Same day to 2 days
* Mandatory publication period: 10 consecutive days
* License release: 10–14 days total processing

---

## 10. Tracking or Status Updates

Applicants may monitor their application through:
* Local Civil Registrar inquiry desks
* LGU SMS or online appointment systems
* Phone or email follow-ups

---

## 11. Common Problems Encountered

Applicants may experience:
- Incorrect PSA records or misspelled names
- Incomplete seminar requirements
- Delayed publication period processing
- Missing parental consent documents
- Expired or invalid IDs

---

## 12. Common Mistakes to Avoid

- Using outdated PSA documents
- Ignoring seminar schedules
- Submitting incomplete requirements
- Applying too close to the wedding date
- Forgetting photocopies of documents and IDs

Always review your application before final submission.

---

## 13. Tips for Faster Processing

* Prepare all PSA documents in advance
* Book seminars early if slots are limited
* Bring multiple photocopies of all documents
* Verify LGU-specific requirements beforehand
* Apply at least 1 month before your wedding date

---

## 14. Frequently Asked Questions

### How long is a Marriage License valid?
A Marriage License is generally valid for 120 days from the date of issue anywhere in the Philippines.

### Is a Marriage License required for church weddings?
Yes. Most churches require a valid Marriage License before conducting the ceremony.

### Can foreigners apply for a Marriage License in the Philippines?
Yes, but additional requirements such as a Certificate of Legal Capacity to Marry may be required.

### Can I apply in a different city?
Applications are usually filed where either applicant resides, depending on LGU rules.

### Is the 10-day waiting period mandatory?
Yes. The publication period is required by Philippine law for most marriage license applications.
