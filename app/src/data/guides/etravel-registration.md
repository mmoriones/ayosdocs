## 1. What is eTravel?

**eTravel** is the unified digital platform for all passengers arriving in or departing from the Philippines. It replaces the traditional paper-based arrival and departure cards.

The eTravel QR Code is commonly used for:
*   Clearing Immigration at all Philippine international airports
*   Health declaration for the Bureau of Quarantine
*   Customs declaration (integrated in some airports)

---

## 2. Who Needs This?

You may need an eTravel QR Code if you are:
- **Arriving in the PH:** All passengers (Filipinos and Foreigners).
- **Departing from the PH:** Only Filipino citizens (including OFWs).
- **Crew Members:** All airline crew entering the Philippines.

---

## 3. Benefits of eTravel Registration

The digital system streamlines the airport experience and removes the need for pens and paper forms.

### Unified Clearance
One QR code covers Immigration, Health (Quarantine), and often Customs, reducing the number of checkpoints.

### Faster Processing
Travelers with a "Green" QR code can use the Express Lanes at the airport.

---

## 4. Requirements

### Primary Requirements
*   **Passport Details:** Passport number and **date of issuance**.
*   **Flight Details:** Airline name and flight number.
*   **Personal Photo:** A clear, recent digital photo of your face (max 5MB).
*   **Email Address:** To receive your confirmation and QR code.

### Additional Requirements (if applicable)
*   **Permanent Address:** Your address in the Philippines or your hotel/accommodation details.

💡 Tip: Registration must be done within **72 hours (3 days)** before your arrival in or departure from the Philippines.

---

## 5. Application Methods

### Official Website
The primary method is via [etravel.gov.ph](https://etravel.gov.ph).

### eGovPH App
You can also register within the **eGovPH** mobile app for a more integrated experience.

---

## 6. Step-by-Step Application Process

### Step 1 – Visit the Portal
Go to [etravel.gov.ph](https://etravel.gov.ph). Be careful of fake sites that look similar but ask for payment.

---

### Step 2 – Choose Travel Type
Select "Arrival" (if entering the Philippines) or "Departure" (if a Filipino citizen leaving).

---

### Step 3 – Enter Personal Details
Provide your name, nationality, passport number, and **date of issuance**. Upload your personal photo when prompted.

---

### Step 4 – Enter Travel Details
Input your date of travel, flight number, airline, and the purpose of your trip (e.g., Tourism, Work, Balikbayan).

---

### Step 5 – Health & Customs Declaration
Answer the brief health questionnaire regarding your recent travel history and any symptoms. If arriving, complete the customs declaration section if applicable.

---

### Step 6 – Save the QR Code
Once submitted, a **QR Code** will be displayed. Take a screenshot or download the PDF. You will also receive a copy via email.

## 10. Tracking or Status Updates

The system provides an instant QR code. There is no "status tracking" needed.
*   **Green QR Code:** Cleared for entry/exit.
*   **Red QR Code:** Proceed to the Bureau of Quarantine/Immigration desk for manual verification.

---

## 11. Common Problems Encountered

Applicants may experience:
- **Website Overload:** The site may be slow during peak travel hours.
- **Photo Upload Fail:** Ensure your photo is a JPG or PNG and under 5MB.
- **Scam Sites:** Sites that look like eTravel but ask for credit card info. **eTravel is always FREE.**

---

## 12. Common Mistakes to Avoid

- **Wrong Flight Number:** Ensure your flight number is correct, as the system validates this against real-time flight data.
- **Registering Too Early:** If you register more than 72 hours before your flight, the QR code may not be valid.

Always have a screenshot of your QR code ready before you land, as airport Wi-Fi can be unreliable.

---

## 13. Tips for Faster Processing

*   Register while you are still at your departure airport or at home to avoid rushing at the arrival gate.
*   If traveling as a family, one person can create an account and add all family members to speed up the process.
*   Keep your passport issuance date handy, as this is a new mandatory field for 2026.

## 15. Frequently Asked Questions

### Do infants need an eTravel QR code?
Yes, every passenger, including children and infants, must have their own individual eTravel QR code.

### Do foreign tourists need a QR code to leave the PH?
No, the departure eTravel registration is currently only mandatory for Filipino citizens. Foreigners only need it for arrival.
