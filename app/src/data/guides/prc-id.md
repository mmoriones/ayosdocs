## 1. What is a PRC ID?

The **PRC ID** (Professional Identification Card) is the official license issued by the Professional Regulation Commission (PRC) to Filipinos who have passed their respective Licensure Examinations (Board Exams). It is recognized as a top-tier **Primary ID** for all legal and financial transactions.

The PRC ID is commonly used for:
*   Legally practicing your profession (Nurse, Teacher, Engineer, etc.)
*   Primary identification for banking and passports
*   Proof of professional standing and ethics

---

## 2. Who Needs This?

You may need a PRC ID if you are:
- A new board exam passer (Initial Registration)
- An existing professional whose license has expired (Renewal)
- A professional needing to update their status or record

---

## 3. Benefits of Having a PRC ID

Beyond being a legal requirement to work, the PRC ID carries significant prestige and utility.

### Professional Authority
It serves as your "license to practice," allowing you to sign official documents and certifications in your field.

### High-Level Identification
It is universally accepted as a Primary ID, often carrying more weight than a basic government ID in high-security transactions.

---

## 4. Requirements

### Primary Requirements
*   **Duly Accomplished Oath Form:** Printed from the LERIS portal.
*   **Notice of Admission (NOA):** From your board exam.
*   **Passport Photos:** Two (2) copies (white background, with name tag).
*   **Documentary Stamps:** Usually 2 sets (available at the PRC office).

### Additional Requirements (if applicable)
*   **Short Brown Envelope:** To hold your documents.
*   **Community Tax Certificate (Cedula):** Required by some regional offices.

💡 Tip: Your 2x2 photo in LERIS must have a **white background** and you must be wearing a **collared shirt**.

---

## 5. Application Methods

### Online (LERIS Portal)
The application, scheduling, and payment are all done via the [PRC LERIS Portal](https://online.prc.gov.ph/).

### Personal Appearance
After the online steps, you **must** personally appear at your chosen PRC office to sign the professional roster and submit documents.

---

## 6. Step-by-Step Application Process

### Step 1 – LERIS Online Registration
Log in to [online.prc.gov.ph](https://online.prc.gov.ph/). Click on **"Initial Registration"** and enter your profession and application number.

---

### Step 2 – Book Appointment and Pay
Select your preferred PRC branch and appointment date. Pay the fees (usually ₱800–₱1,050) via GCash, Maya, or Credit Card.

---

### Step 3 – Print Forms
Download and print your **Oath Form** and **Appointment Slip** from the "Existing Transactions" tab.

---

### Step 4 – Attend Oath-Taking
You must participate in the mass or special oath-taking ceremony (face-to-face or virtual as scheduled).

---

### Step 5 – Visit PRC for ID Issuance
On your appointment date, go to the PRC office. Submit your documents and sign the Professional Registry. You will typically receive your PRC ID on the same day or be given a claiming schedule.

---

## 7. Where to Apply

Applicants may process their PRC ID at:
- [PRC LERIS Online Portal](https://online.prc.gov.ph/)
- **PRC Regional Offices:** (Manila, Cebu, Davao, Baguio, etc.)
- **PRC Service Centers:** (Robinsons Malls and SM Malls)

## 9. Processing Time

Typical processing timelines include:
*   Online Step: 15 Minutes
*   Personal Appearance: 1–4 Hours (Same Day)

---

## 10. Tracking or Status Updates

You can track your application status in the **"Existing Transactions"** section of your LERIS account.

---

## 11. Common Problems Encountered

Applicants may experience:
- **No Record Found:** Ensure you entered the correct application number from your NOA.
- **Payment Not Reflecting:** If the system doesn't update, wait 24 hours before trying again to avoid double payment.

---

## 12. Common Mistakes to Avoid

- **Incomplete Requirements:** Forgetting documentary stamps or brown envelopes can cause delays.
- **Wrong Branch:** Going to a different PRC branch than the one you selected in LERIS.

Always check the "Mandatory Requirements" for your specific profession, as some (like Medicine or Law) have additional steps.

---

## 13. Tips for Faster Processing

*   Book appointments at PRC Service Centers in malls; they are often faster and less crowded than regional offices.
*   Keep a digital copy of your NOA and Oath Form on your phone.
*   Buy your documentary stamps in advance if you want to skip the line inside the PRC.

---

## 14. Frequently Asked Questions

### How long is the PRC ID valid?
It is valid for **three (3) years** and is renewable on your birth month.

### Can I send a representative for initial registration?
Generally, no. Initial registration requires a personal signature in the professional roster (signing the book). Renewals, however, can be done by a representative with an SPA.
