## 1. What is the Authority to Print (ATP)?

The **Authority to Print (ATP)** is a permit issued by the BIR that allows a business to print its own official Invoices and Receipts through an accredited printer. 

**Note for 2026:** Under the **Ease of Paying Taxes (EOPT) Act**, the **"Invoice"** is now the primary document for both sales of goods and services. The traditional "Official Receipt" is now a supplementary document.

The ATP and Invoices are commonly used for:
*   Issuing legal proof of sale to customers
*   Claiming Input VAT (for VAT-registered buyers)
*   Substantiating business income and expenses during audits

---

## 2. Who Needs This?

You may need an ATP if you are:
- A new business owner (Sole Proprietor or Corporation)
- A freelancer or self-employed professional
- An existing business whose current invoice booklets are running out
- An existing business transitioning from "Official Receipts" to "Invoices" under the new law

---

## 3. Benefits of Having Official Invoices

Official invoices are the backbone of a legal business in the Philippines.

### Professionalism and Trust
Issuing a BIR-registered invoice builds trust with corporate clients who need these documents for their own tax deductions.

### Avoidance of Penalties
Failure to issue an official invoice for sales can result in fines starting at ₱10,000 per violation.

---

## 4. Requirements

### Primary Requirements
*   **BIR Form 1906:** Application for Authority to Print.
*   **Certificate of Registration (Form 2303):** Photocopy of your COR.
*   **Sample Layout:** A draft of your proposed Invoice showing mandatory fields (Business name, TIN, Address, etc.).

### Printer Details
*   **Accredited Printer:** You must have the Name, TIN, and Accreditation Number of a BIR-authorized printing shop.

💡 Tip: Most accredited printers will help you with the layout and even the ATP application process as part of their service.

---

## 5. Application Methods

### Online (ORUS)
You can apply for the ATP digitally via the [BIR ORUS Portal](https://orus.bir.gov.ph).

### Walk-In (RDO)
Submit your Form 1906 and requirements to the Revenue District Office (RDO) where your business is registered.

---

## 6. Step-by-Step Application Process

### Step 1 – Find an Accredited Printer
Look for a printer with a "BIR Accredited" sign. Request a quote for printing (usually sold in sets of 10 or 50 booklets).

---

### Step 2 – Create the Layout
Work with the printer to create your invoice layout. Ensure it includes:
*   "Invoice" title (not "Official Receipt")
*   Your Business Name, Address, and TIN (with Branch Code)
*   Space for Customer Name, Address, and TIN
*   VAT breakdown (if applicable)

---

### Step 3 – File BIR Form 1906
Log in to **ORUS** or visit your **RDO**. Submit the application and upload/attach the sample layout and your COR.

---

### Step 4 – Receive the ATP (Form 1921)
Once approved, the BIR will issue the **Authority to Print (Form 1921)**. 

---

### Step 5 – Printing and Delivery
Give the ATP to your printer. They will print your booklets and must submit a **Printer’s Certificate of Delivery (PCD)** to the BIR within 30 days. You can then pick up your official booklets.

---

## 7. Where to Apply

Applicants may process their ATP at:
- [BIR ORUS Portal](https://orus.bir.gov.ph)
- Your assigned **Revenue District Office (RDO)**

If applying via ORUS, you can check the "Application Status" tab in your dashboard. If manual, you will need to follow up with your RDO or the printer.

---

## 11. Common Problems Encountered

Applicants may experience:
- **Layout Rejection:** If mandatory information is missing or the text is too small.
- **Printer Delay:** Printers may have a backlog, especially during peak registration seasons (January).

---

## 12. Common Mistakes to Avoid

- **Using "Official Receipt" for Services:** Ensure your new booklets say **"Invoice"** to comply with 2026 EOPT rules.
- **Printing without ATP:** Never print receipts or invoices through a non-accredited shop; they will not be legally valid.

Always check the serial numbers on your printed booklets against the ATP to ensure they match.

---

## 13. Tips for Faster Processing

*   Ask your printer if they can handle the BIR filing for you.
*   Ensure your COR is updated and shows the correct "Registered Activity."
*   Print at least 10–20 booklets at once to save on the per-unit cost.

---

## 14. Frequently Asked Questions

### Can I use generic "Receipt" booklets from bookstores?
No. For a registered business, only invoices/receipts printed with an official ATP and serial numbers are valid for tax purposes.

### Do Invoices expire?
Invoices no longer have a 5-year expiration date (as of RR 6-2022). You can use them until the booklets are consumed.
