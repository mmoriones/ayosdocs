## 1. What is PSA CENOMAR?

A PSA CENOMAR (Certificate of No Marriage Record) is an official document issued by the Philippine Statistics Authority (PSA) certifying that a person has no registered marriage in the Philippine civil registry database.

It is commonly required when applying for a marriage license, visa, employment abroad, loans, or other legal transactions requiring proof of civil status.

The PSA CENOMAR is commonly used for:
* Marriage license applications
* Visa and immigration requirements
* Employment or overseas work applications

---

## 2. Who Needs This?

You may need a PSA CENOMAR if you are:
- Applying for a marriage license in the Philippines
- Processing visa or immigration documents
- Applying for certain government or private sector requirements

---

## 3. Benefits of Having PSA CENOMAR

### Proof of Civil Status
The document officially certifies that you have no registered marriage record under the PSA database.

### Required for Marriage Applications
Most Local Civil Registry Offices require a CENOMAR before issuing a marriage license.

### Accepted for Legal Transactions
Embassies, employers, banks, and government agencies commonly accept PSA-issued CENOMAR documents.

---

## 4. Requirements

### Primary Requirements
* Valid government-issued ID
* Complete personal information of the document owner

Required information usually includes:
* Full name
* Date of birth
* Place of birth
* Father's full name
* Mother's maiden name
* Purpose of request

### Additional Requirements (if applicable)
* Authorization letter if requesting for another person
* Valid IDs of both requester and document owner
* Proof of relationship (for immediate family requests)

💡 Tip: Ensure your personal details exactly match your PSA records to avoid “No Record Found” issues.

---

## 5. Application Methods

Applicants may request a PSA CENOMAR through online services or walk-in applications.

### Online Application
Applicants may request online through:
* PSAHelpline
* PSA Serbilis
* PSA e-Certificate services

Documents may be delivered to your home address or provided digitally (if available).

### Walk-In Application
Applicants may process requests at PSA Civil Registry System (CRS) outlets and selected SM Business Centers.

Most PSA outlets require online appointment booking before visiting.

## 14. Frequently Asked Questions

### Is a CENOMAR required for marriage in the Philippines?
Yes. Most Local Civil Registry Offices require a PSA CENOMAR before issuing a marriage license.

### Can I request a CENOMAR for another person?
Yes, but additional requirements such as authorization letters and IDs may be required.

### How long is a PSA CENOMAR valid?
Validity depends on the requesting institution. Many offices accept documents issued within the last 6 months.

### Can I get a digital copy of my CENOMAR?
Yes. PSA now offers e-Certificate services for eligible applicants.

### What happens if I previously had a marriage record?
Instead of a CENOMAR, PSA may issue an Advisory on Marriage.
