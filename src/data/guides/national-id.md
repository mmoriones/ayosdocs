## 1. What is the Philippine National ID?

The **Philippine Identification System (PhilSys) National ID** is the official national identification system of the Philippines.

It provides every Filipino citizen and resident alien with a single, secure, and verifiable identity.

The National ID may be issued in different formats:

* Physical National ID card (PhilID)
* Paper-based PhilSys ePhilID
* Digital National ID

The system is managed by the **Philippine Statistics Authority (PSA)** under Republic Act No. 11055 or the PhilSys Act.

The National ID can be used for:

* Identity verification
* Government transactions
* Opening bank accounts
* School and employment requirements
* SIM registration
* Accessing public and private services
* Financial services and benefits

The National ID is considered a valid proof of identity nationwide.

---

## 2. Who Can Apply?

You may register for the National ID if you are:

- A Filipino citizen
- A resident alien living in the Philippines
- At least 5 years old
- A parent or guardian registering a child

Children below 5 years old may also be registered, but their biometrics will be updated when they reach the required age.

---

## 3. Benefits of Having a National ID

Having a National ID provides several advantages:

### Easier Transactions

The National ID simplifies identity verification across government agencies and private institutions.

### Faster Access to Services

It may be used for:

* Banking
* Social welfare programs
* Government assistance
* Healthcare services
* Public records requests

### Reduced Need for Multiple IDs

Instead of carrying several government-issued IDs, you can use one unified identification system.

### Digital Verification

The Digital National ID allows online and electronic identity verification.

---

## 4. Requirements

### Primary Documents

Applicants must present at least one official document showing:

* Full name
* Date of birth
* Photograph or biometric information

Accepted documents may include:

* PSA-issued Birth Certificate
* Passport
* Driver’s License
* UMID
* SSS ID
* GSIS ID
* PRC ID
* Voter’s ID
* PhilHealth ID

### For Minors

Additional requirements may include:

* PSA birth certificate
* Parent or guardian’s National ID or valid ID

### For Resident Aliens

Resident aliens may need:

* Alien Certificate of Registration (ACR)
* Valid immigration documents

💡 Tip: Bring both original and photocopies of your documents.

## 5. Registration Methods

There are several ways to register for the National ID.

### Walk-In Registration

Applicants may visit designated PhilSys registration centers.

### Online Appointment or Pre-Registration

Some registration centers may allow online pre-registration to reduce waiting time.

### Mobile Registration

Certain local government units and remote areas may conduct mobile registration drives.

---

## 6. Step-by-Step Registration Process

### Step 1 – Prepare Your Documents

Gather all required identification documents before visiting the registration center.

Ensure that:

* Names are spelled correctly
* Birth dates match all records
* Documents are clear and readable

Incorrect information may delay processing.

---

### Step 2 – Visit a PhilSys Registration Center

Go to the nearest registration center.

These may include:

* PSA offices
* Selected malls
* Local government offices
* Government service centers

Bring your required documents.

---

### Step 3 – Fill Out the Registration Form

You will provide personal details such as:

* Full name
* Sex
* Date of birth
* Address
* Blood type (optional)
* Contact information

Double-check all information before submission.

---

### Step 4 – Document Verification

Staff will verify your documents and personal information.

Any discrepancies may require additional supporting documents.

---

### Step 5 – Biometric Capture

Your biometric information will be collected, including:

* Photograph
* Fingerprints
* Iris scan

Children below 5 years old may only have a photograph taken initially.

---

### Step 6 – Review and Confirm Information

Before final submission, carefully review all encoded details.

Check for:

* Correct spelling
* Accurate birth date
* Updated address
* Proper formatting

Once confirmed, your registration will be finalized.

---

### Step 7 – Receive Transaction Slip

You will receive a transaction or reference slip.

Keep this document safe since it may be needed for tracking or verification.

---

### Step 8 – Wait for Delivery or Access the Digital ID

After processing, you may receive:

* Physical National ID card
* Paper-based ePhilID
* Digital National ID access

Delivery timelines may vary depending on your location and application volume.

## 14. Frequently Asked Questions

### Is the National ID free?

Yes. Initial registration and first issuance are free of charge.

### Can minors apply?

Yes. Children may be registered with the assistance of a parent or guardian.

### Is the Digital National ID valid?

Yes. The Digital National ID may be accepted for many transactions and identity verification purposes.

### What if I lose my National ID?

You may request a replacement, subject to verification and applicable replacement procedures.

### Can I use the National ID for banking?

Yes. Many banks and financial institutions accept the National ID as valid proof of identity.

## 15. Summary

The Philippine National ID is a unified government-issued identification system designed to simplify identity verification and improve access to services.

To apply, prepare your documents, visit a registration center, complete biometric capture, verify your details, and wait for card delivery or digital ID activation.

The National ID can be used for government services, banking, employment, education, and everyday identity verification across the Philippines.
